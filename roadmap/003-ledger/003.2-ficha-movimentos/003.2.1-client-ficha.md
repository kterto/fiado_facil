# 003.2.1 — Client ficha and history (T3) [mvp] [delivery]

## Brief
Build the client ficha (`docs/design/07-ficha-cliente.html`) without the cancel and statement actions (they come in later stories). Read `.orchestrator/PROJECT-CONTEXT.md` (Invariants, Conventions, Commands) and `docs/foundation/INTENT.md` / `ACTORS.md` before starting.

- Header: name, phone, balance label and value in 32, limit marker "Limite: R$ X (passou)" from the domain rules.
- Buttons "Fiado" (primary), "Pagou", "Enviar extrato" (navigate to stubs for now).
- "Histórico": compras and pagamentos merged, newest first, 30 per page with load-more; items "Compra + R$ …" / "Pagamento − R$ …" with meta "dd/mm · descrição · operador" (operador = `usuarios` nome of `registrado_por`); cancelled items struck through with "Cancelado por {nome}"; "Ainda não enviado" marker for pending writes; empty state "Nenhuma compra registrada ainda.".
- Saldo is computed from all of the client's records, independent of history pagination.
- Must meet: ficha visible in ≤ 2 s on a mid-range device with cached data.
