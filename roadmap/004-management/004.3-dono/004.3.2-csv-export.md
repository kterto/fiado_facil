# 004.3.2 — CSV export (owner only) [mvp] [delivery]

## Brief
Let the owner export all data as CSV through the Android share sheet. Only visible to the dono. Read `.orchestrator/PROJECT-CONTEXT.md` (Invariants, Conventions, Commands) and `docs/foundation/INTENT.md` / `ACTORS.md` before starting. There is no design file for this screen. Build it only from the existing design system (tokens, type scale, components and patterns in `docs/design/*.html` and the app theme), and add a visible note in the story's output summary that the screen **needs design validation** by the product owner.

- Three files: `clientes.csv`, `lancamentos.csv`, `pagamentos.csv` with every field (including inactive clients and cancelled entries), money as centavos plus a formatted R$ column, dates ISO-8601 and `dd/mm/aaaa`.
- UTF-8 with BOM and `;` separator so Excel/Sheets in pt-BR open them correctly; names include the export date.
- Share via `share_plus` (Drive, WhatsApp to self, e-mail). The app never reads the CSV back.
