# 005.1.2 — Accessibility and performance pass [mvp] [delivery]

## Brief
Audit and fix accessibility and performance across all screens. Read `.orchestrator/PROJECT-CONTEXT.md` (Invariants, Conventions, Commands) and `docs/foundation/INTENT.md` / `ACTORS.md` before starting.

- TalkBack labels for every control and amount; never color alone (labels like "Deve"/"Crédito" always present); usable at 200% font scale with no clipped text; contrast ≥ 4.5:1; touch targets ≥ 48dp.
- Measure on a profile build: ficha visible ≤ 2 s, save confirmation ≤ 1 s, on a 2 GB-RAM Android 10 device or emulator with slow 4G (1.5 Mbps / 300 ms). Record the numbers.
